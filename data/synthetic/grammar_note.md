# Nadi-9 grammar note

SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA. Nadi-9 is fictional and was written for this exercise. It is not a real language.

author: Training desk
date: 2026-02-01
reliability: 0.75
scope: word order, tense, negation, respect, code-switching. The question particle is intentionally unfinished.

## rule word-order
id: word-order
Statements put the subject before the object, and the verb last.

## rule tense-past
id: tense-past
Past tense is the verb stem plus va.

## rule tense-present
id: tense-present
Present tense is the verb stem plus ra.

## rule tense-future
id: tense-future
Future tense is the verb stem plus mi.

## rule tense-imperative
id: tense-imperative
When a higher-status speaker gives a command, use the bare verb stem.

## rule negation
id: negation
Put na immediately before the verb.

## rule respect
id: respect
When the speaker is lower status than the addressee, put sha immediately before the verb. Do not add sha when there is no verb. The kinship noun orun is already respectful.

## rule back
id: back
The particle reth sits immediately after the verb and means back or again.

## rule copula
id: copula
A present-state sentence uses ri after the adjective.

## rule code-switch
id: code-switch
These English tech nouns stay in English spelling: wifi, phone.

## rule question-particle
id: question-particle
This note does not choose the question particle. Specialists disagree, so do not guess.

## rule place-time
id: place-time
Time words sit before the verb.

## rule place-vocative
id: place-vocative
A comma address, such as a kinship term, is spoken after the verb group and before a question particle.

## rule you-form
id: you-form
The word for you depends on the addressee. Higher addressee: shu. Peer or lower addressee: tu.
